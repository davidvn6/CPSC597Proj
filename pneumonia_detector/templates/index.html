About the Model & Evaluation

How It Works: ResNet-50 first learned image features from a large image collection. It was then trained on this project's chest X-Rays to classify images as normal or pneumonia.

How the prediction is chosen: A pneumonia score at or above the model's cutoff (approximately —) produces the pneumonia class. A lower score produces the normal class. This cutoff was chosen using validation images before final testing.

What the metrics mean: Accuracy is the percentage of test images classified correctly. Pneumonia recall measures how many pneumonia images were detected. Specificity measures how many normal images were classified correctly. F1 combines precision and recall. ROC-AUC measures how well the model separates the two classes across possible cutoffs.
